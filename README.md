Trip Logbook – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 10 November 2026

Trip Logbook is a vehicle logbook app for recording business and private trips. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your logbook stays on your phone
Trips, vehicles, odometer readings, notes and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
The app has no account system and no server of its own. We never receive your logbook.
Reports (PDF or CSV) leave your device only when you choose to share them, to the app you pick.
Android's own device backup may include app data if you have it switched on in your phone's settings.
Location
The app reads your precise location only while you are recording a trip that you started by tapping “Start trip”. A notification is shown the whole time.
Location is used on the device to measure the distance driven and to suggest the start and end place names. Your phone's built-in geocoder may contact its provider (usually Google) to turn coordinates into a place name.
The route itself is not stored. Only the distance and the place names you keep are saved, on your device.
The app does not collect location in the background when no trip is being recorded, and we never receive your location.
Advertising
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information.
Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Pro removes all ads and the ads software is no longer started.

Purchases
The Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; the app only learns whether the purchase is active. We never see your payment details.

Children
The app is intended for adult drivers and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data
You can delete any trip or vehicle inside the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Not tax advice
Claim figures shown in the app are estimates based on the rate you enter. The app is a record-keeping tool and does not provide tax advice.
